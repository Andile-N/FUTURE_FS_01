    • About me

    I am an undergraduate Computer Science student with a growing interest in both web development and Business Analysis. This year strengthened my passion for Information Systems after learning about the role of Business Analysts, the tools they use, and concepts such as the SDLC, use case diagrams, class diagrams, BACCM, and business process analysis. One of my most rewarding experiences was working on a group project that proposed a digital solution for a local business affected by the COVID-19 pandemic. The experience deepened my interest in solving real-world business problems through technology. Alongside web development, I have also enjoyed working with statistical programming tools such as SAS and R, which introduced me to the analytical side of technology and data. I aim to continue growing my skills in both development and Business Analysis while exploring how technology can improve business processes and user experiences.
